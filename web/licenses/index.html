The WebAssembly modules

The pages of this site run two WebAssembly modules compiled from Wiz by Emscripten: playground/wiz-playground.wasm (the playground, built by web/build-playground.sh) and docs/wiz-infoview.wasm (the infoview of the documentation pages, built by web/build.sh).

Both include mini-gmp from GNU MP (GMP) 6.3.0, linked statically, under the GNU LGPL v3 or later, or the GNU GPL v2 or later: LGPL-3.0.txt (the LGPL v3) and GPL-3.0.txt (the GPL v3, which the LGPL v3 adds to). Its source: https://gmplib.org/ (mini-gmp is the mini-gmp/ directory of the GMP 6.3.0 release tarball).

Because Wiz is itself under the GPL, with its complete source at https://github.com/wiz-pl/wiz, you can rebuild the modules against a modified mini-gmp: web/build.sh OUT --gmp-src GMP.tar.xz and web/build-playground.sh OUT --gmp-src GMP.tar.xz take mini-gmp from the tarball you give them.

The release tarball's notice for the toolchain itself is share/wiz/NOTICE (scripts/release-licenses/NOTICE).
